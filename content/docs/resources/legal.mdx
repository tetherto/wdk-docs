## Third-party services

Some WDK modules call services run by third parties, such as price feeds, fiat on-ramps and swap routers. WDK is open source software. It does not grant you any right to use those services or the data they return.

Each provider sets its own terms. Those terms can restrict commercial use or the redistribution of data, even when the service needs no account or API key. Review the provider's terms before you ship, and make sure they allow your use.

| [`@tetherto/wdk-pricing-bitfinex-http`](/tools/price-rates) | Bitfinex | [Market Data Terms](https://www.bitfinex.com/legal/general/market-data/) |

Community modules can call third-party services too. The same applies to them.
