Install the [published package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tetherto/wdk-wallet-multisig-safe/v/1.0.0-beta.1), then follow the task that matches your Safe's state.

<Cards>
<Card title="Get Started" href="/sdk/wallet-modules/wallet-multisig-safe/guides/get-started">Install beta.1 and connect an owner to an existing Safe.</Card>
<Card title="Deploy a Safe" href="/sdk/wallet-modules/wallet-multisig-safe/guides/deploy-safe">Predict a Safe address and fund its owner's deployment transaction.</Card>
<Card title="Manage Proposals" href="/sdk/wallet-modules/wallet-multisig-safe/guides/manage-proposals">Quote, propose, approve and submit a transfer.</Card>
<Card title="Manage Owners" href="/sdk/wallet-modules/wallet-multisig-safe/guides/manage-owners">Propose owner and threshold changes.</Card>
<Card title="Sign Messages" href="/sdk/wallet-modules/wallet-multisig-safe/guides/sign-messages">Collect Safe message confirmations and verify a combined signature.</Card>
<Card title="Coordinate Approvals" href="/sdk/wallet-modules/wallet-multisig-safe/guides/coordinate-approvals">Configure Safe Transaction Service or your own coordinator.</Card>
<Card title="Handle Errors" href="/sdk/wallet-modules/wallet-multisig-safe/guides/handle-errors">Interpret failures and clear signing material.</Card>
</Cards>

The examples use independently supplied RPC, Safe, owner and recipient values. Check every address and chain before signing. Proposal approval and execution are separate actions unless the documented auto-execution condition applies.
