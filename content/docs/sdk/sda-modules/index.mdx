Smart Deposit Address (SDA) modules let an application discover deposit routes and create deposit addresses through a provider. A depositor sends an ordinary transfer to the address; the provider handles its supported routing and settlement flow.

## Choose a provider

| Module | Provider | Ownership | Address model | Availability | Documentation |
|--------|----------|-----------|---------------|--------------|---------------|
| [`@candidelabs/wdk-protocol-sda-candide`](https://github.com/candidelabs/wdk-protocol-sda-candide) | Candide | Community | Deterministic contract address with recipient withdrawal and optional recovery permissions. | npm publication pending | [Overview](/sdk/sda-modules/sda-candide/) |

Provider custody and available operations differ. Review the provider's contract, activation expiry, and recovery permissions before displaying an address to a depositor.

## Understand the shared interface

SDA providers extend `SdaProtocol`. Route discovery and address creation are required; quote, lookup, history, disable, derivation, renewal, and recovery operations depend on the provider. An optional method can throw `UnsupportedOperationError` when the provider does not implement it.

Register a compatible provider with `wdk.registerProtocol()` and retrieve it from an account with [`getSdaProtocol()`](/sdk/core-module/api-reference#getsdaprotocollabel). Some providers also support direct construction without a wallet account. Follow their usage pages for the exact constructor, credentials, and supported methods.

Discover routes at runtime rather than assuming a chain or asset is available. Preserve the address's chain, asset restrictions, destination, and any required memo when instructing a depositor. Address creation and deposit completion are separate events; use the provider's documented transfer-tracking flow to reconcile deliveries.
