Smart Deposit Address (SDA) modules let an application discover deposit routes and issue deposit addresses through a provider. A depositor sends an ordinary transfer to the issued address; the provider handles its supported routing and settlement flow.

## Choose a provider

| Module | Provider | Ownership | Custody model | Documentation |
|--------|----------|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| [`@rhino.fi/wdk-protocol-sda-rhinofi`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@rhino.fi/wdk-protocol-sda-rhinofi) | Rhino.fi | Community | Trusted operator: Rhino.fi issues and sweeps the deposit addresses. | [Overview](/sdk/sda-modules/sda-rhinofi/) |

Provider custody and available operations differ. Rhino.fi manages funds while routing a deposit, even though its WDK module does not sign transactions or access private keys. Read the provider's limitations before displaying a deposit address to a user.

## Understand the shared interface

SDA providers extend `SdaProtocol`. Route discovery and address creation are required; quote, lookup, history, disable, derivation, renewal, and recovery operations depend on the provider. An optional method can throw `UnsupportedOperationError` when the provider does not implement it.

Register a compatible provider with `wdk.registerProtocol()` and retrieve it from an account with [`getSdaProtocol()`](/sdk/core-module/api-reference#getsdaprotocollabel). Some providers also support direct construction without a wallet account. Follow their usage pages for the exact constructor, credentials, and supported methods.

Discover routes at runtime rather than assuming a chain or asset is available. Preserve the issued address's chain, asset restrictions, destination, and any required memo when instructing a depositor. Address issuance and deposit completion are separate events; use the provider's documented history or webhook flow to reconcile transfers.
